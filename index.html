About CrimeVision

Crime prediction is a vital tool in modern law enforcement and urban planning. By analyzing historical patterns and leveraging advanced machine learning techniques, we can anticipate crime trends and empower communities and authorities to take proactive measures for public safety.

CrimeVision AI is built on a comprehensive dataset sourced from the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB). It covers 19 major Indian metropolitan cities with data spanning from 2014 to 2021, encompassing 10 distinct crime categories — from cyber crimes and economic offences to crimes against women and senior citizens.

Our prediction engine uses a Random Forest Regression model trained on this rich dataset, achieving an impressive accuracy of 93.20%. The model considers city-specific patterns, crime category trends, and temporal dynamics to deliver reliable forecasts that can guide policy and resource allocation.
